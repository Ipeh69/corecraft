About CoreCraft

Build with confidence.

CoreCraft is a PC-building assistant made for Filipino builders. It brings compatibility checks, build recommendations, local shop discovery, pricing guidance, troubleshooting help, and community discussion into one place.

What we provide

  • Compatibility guidance for CPUs, motherboards, memory, GPUs, storage, and power supplies.
  • Budget-conscious build recommendations for gaming, school, work, streaming, and editing.
  • Location-based shop discovery across the Philippines.
  • Practical AI assistance powered by the information you provide.

Important note

Prices and stock change frequently. Always confirm availability, warranty, compatibility, and final pricing with the seller before purchasing.
